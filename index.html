Rule of thumb — SHA256 ratio ≥ 20% or ARM64 share ≥ 30% are strong automation signals. Windows / macOS are excluded because their checksums are pulled by Winget / Homebrew validators and would skew the ratio. Since v2.3.1 a multi-arch GHCR image is also available; CI / container automation that previously fetched linux-*.tar.gz here may shift to docker pull over time, gradually lowering the signals shown above.

Docker / GHCR

Multi-arch image at ghcr.io/m-igashi/mp3rgain, available since v2.3.1. Designed as a drop-in for the classic mp3gain Docker workflows used by Plex / Jellyfin / cron-driven media servers.
